The Challenge: Overcoming Taste Hesitation & Explaining Herbal Cleansing

Traditional herbal tonics and bitters (like Soursop and Moringa Oleifera) have a centuries-old history of holistic digestive health and immune defense. However, modern Amazon shoppers often hesitate due to taste concerns, unclear dosage instructions, or unfamiliarity with wildcrafted ingredients.

Amenazel and Moringa Bitters needed high-impact visual architectures to clarify how their 21-herb organic blend works, reassure buyers regarding natural purity, and showcase easy 3-step daily usage.

Visual Strategy: Botanical Authenticity Meets Modern Clarity

We engineered an earth-toned, deeply vibrant visual system emphasizing raw botanicals:

  • Hero Graviola & Moringa Artwork: Showcased whole soursop fruit, fresh moringa leaves, and authentic Jamaican ginger to ground the product in real agriculture.
  • 21 Herbs & Roots Visual Grid: Organized complex botanical ingredients into an easy-to-digest infographic detailing specific roles (colon cleanse, joint vitality, immune strength).
  • Step-by-Step Daily Usage Guide: Created an intuitive 1-2-3 guide (Shake, Measure 2 Tbsp, Enjoy in warm water or tea) to reduce user anxiety.

Live Result on Amazon

The unified design suite helped the brand achieve a +35% sales uplift and rapid adoption across international Amazon marketplaces (US & Saudi Arabia).
