Workflow

Our Listing Design Process

How my team and I deliver a structured, data-driven approach focusing on customer psychology and competitive advantages.

01

Competitor Research

Analyze reviews, identify negative complaints on competing listings, and figure out the exact angles that will win customer trust.

02

Visual Storyboarding

Outline listing images (Main, Infographic, Lifestyle) and plan the modules sequence for A+ content to keep customers scrolling.

03

AI & Photoshop Magic

Generate highly realistic lifestyle background scenes with Midjourney/Flux, and polish/blend the product packaging manually in Photoshop.

04

Deliver & Optimize

Deliver premium, Amazon-compliant files ready for upload. Provide post-launch support and image updates for split testing.
