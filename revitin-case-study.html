The Challenge: Explaining Prebiotic Oral Science in 3 Seconds

Most commercial toothpastes rely on harsh antimicrobial agents that destroy both good and bad bacteria. Revitin, formulated by holistic dentists, introduced a revolutionary natural formula that feeds the good oral microbiome with Vitamins C, D, E, K, ionic minerals, and CoQ10.

However, when shoppers searched for "natural fluoride-free toothpaste" on Amazon, Revitin was competing with hundreds of generic brands. Their clinical terminology was too complex for casual smartphone shoppers. Toufiq Creative was tasked with redesigning both the Listing Image Stack and the A+ Content (EBC) to turn microbiome science into instant emotional buying appeal.

Part 1: The High-Converting Listing Image Architecture

We engineered a visual hierarchy that balanced radiant lifestyle photography with clean nutritional callouts:

Lifestyle Callout: Warm, genuine lifestyle photography reinforcing natural, chemical-free oral wellness.
  • Hero Main Lifestyle: Paired real human smile emotion with real citrus fruits to communicate freshness instantly.
  • Microbiome Balancing Graphic: Designed an intuitive diagram showing how prebiotics nurture beneficial bacteria while crowding out pathogens.
  • Clean Nutrition Badges: Prominently featured non-toxic, SLS-free, fluoride-free, and swallow-safe badges for whole family reassurance.

Part 2: 7-Module Editorial A+ Content (EBC)

Below the fold, our A+ Content design bridged the gap between premium lifestyle branding and clinical credibility:

  • Citrus Splash Hero Banner: Full-width responsive graphic with sliced oranges and tangerines highlighting refreshing natural taste.
  • Ingredient Transparency Breakdown: Clear visual cards showcasing CoQ10 for gum health, Vitamin C for tissue repair, and cranberry extract for plaque defense.
  • Traditional vs. Revitin Comparison Chart: A head-to-head comparison highlighting zero synthetic dyes, zero abrasives, and zero SLS.

Live Result on Amazon

Following the visual rollout, Revitin experienced a +39% increase in click-through rate and a +34% conversion rate boost across their single tubes and multi-packs on Amazon US.
