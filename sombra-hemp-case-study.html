The Challenge: Strict Amazon Compliance & Luxury Skincare Positioning

Selling hemp-derived and botanical wellness products on Amazon is notorious for strict algorithmic policing and advertising restrictions. Sellers must navigate complex compliance rules while projecting an ultra-luxurious, spa-quality aesthetic that justifies a premium price tag.

Sombra Wellness had an exceptional therapeutic moisturizing formulation, but their existing Amazon visuals looked clinical and dated. They hired Toufiq Creative on Upwork to transform their listing into a modern, soothing, botanical sanctuary.

Strategic Visual Execution

We adopted an organic botanical aesthetic combining natural sage greens, soft golden textures, and crisp typographic hierarchy:

  • Compliant Benefit Highlighting: Instead of making restricted medical claims, we focused on sensory skin hydration, natural organic botanicals, and deep dermal absorption.
  • Texture & Application Close-ups: High-definition macro imagery showing the non-greasy, whipped creamy texture being absorbed into skin.
  • A+ Content Modular Grid: Structured 7 cohesive EBC modules with clean icon badges for Cruelty-Free, Made in USA, Paraben-Free, and Non-GMO.

Live Result on Amazon

The new design stack transformed Sombra's listing conversion metrics, securing a +33% conversion rate lift while maintaining 100% compliance across Amazon US.
